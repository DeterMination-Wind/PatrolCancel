**Patrol Cancel** is a lightweight client mod for Mindustry v8 (vanilla and MindustryX).

## Right-click cancels patrol

Right-clicking to command your selected units automatically clears their Patrol stance.

In vanilla v8, right-click commands only move units and never touch the Patrol stance, so patrolling units get dragged back into their old patrol loop on arrival — they look stuck and ignore the move order. This mod clears the Patrol stance on your selected, commandable units before every plain right-click command, then lets the vanilla move / attack command fire normally — your units obey immediately and never get stuck in the patrol loop again.
